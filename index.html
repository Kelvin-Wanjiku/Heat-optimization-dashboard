    Scenarios

    The twin simulated four control scenarios; this dashboard charts the two extremes of that story.

    Baseline ("Today") — the building's actual, undiagnosed control faults: roughly half the radiator thermostatic valves stuck open, an outdoor-temperature sensor reading about 1.5 °C low with a one-hour lag, and a manual 2 °C morning boost layered on top. Optimized ("Smart control") — coordinated smart-valve control with those faults fixed. Two more scenarios exist in the raw data but aren't charted here yet — "faults fixed, same old controller" and "smart control running on the old faulty valves"

    Room energy balance

    The twin's own heat model runs on this equation — the contract agreed with the heat-model owner, not a display-only diagram.

    C · dTin/dt = Qradiator + Qinternal + Qsolar − (Tin − Tout)/R − Qvent

    Qradiator: twin, from valve position & supply temperature, capped at the 3 kW rated output. Qinternal: pupils + teacher + lighting + equipment while occupied. Qsolar: irradiance × window area × glazing g-value. Qvent: ventilation loss, net of heat-recovery efficiency — the lever the "optimized" scenario uses. Full term-by-term definitions: data/room_energy_balance.md.
